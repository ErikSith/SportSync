info Event Overview

The Alpine Route Pro Circuit is an uncompromising test of endurance and tactical acumen. Spanning 145 kilometers of punishing gradients and high-altitude switchbacks, this event separates elite competitors from the rest.

Competitors will face three Hors Catégorie (HC) climbs, unpredictable mountain weather systems, and technically demanding descents that require absolute precision. This is a self-supported stage; riders are expected to carry necessary nutrition and emergency maintenance gear.
