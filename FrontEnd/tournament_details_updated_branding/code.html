Rules & Regulations

1. Conduct & Dress Code

Strict adherence to the Apex Elite dress code is mandatory. Competitors must wear approved performance gear in predominantly monochrome (black, white, or charcoal). No visible oversized sponsor logos permitted outside of designated gear zones. Unsportsmanlike conduct results in immediate expulsion without refund.

2. Match Format

All preliminary rounds are best of three sets, standard tiebreak at 6-6. Semifinals and Finals will utilize the Apex No-Ad scoring system to maintain high intensity and broadcast pacing.

3. Equipment & Check-in

Players must check-in to the Telemetry Desk 60 minutes prior to match start. Rackets will be scanned and verified for compliance. Late arrivals face a 1-game penalty per 5 minutes late.

4. Medical Timeouts

Limited to one 3-minute medical evaluation per match, strictly enforced by the tournament's on-site sports medicine director.
